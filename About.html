Hostel Rules

  • You must pay your fees in full and insist on collecting a receipt as proof of payment. Payments are not refundable.
  • The hostel fee excludes Two Hundred Ghana cedis (GHC 200) as a security deposit against damages. This would be refunded when management is satisfied that everything in the rooms, toilets, bathrooms, and the environment is in good state. All students are entitled to free room service (sweeping, mopping, and cleaning) at least twice in a semester. Any extra service required would be paid for.
  • You must not, for any reason, leave your room or wardrobe unlocked. Keep your belongings out of sight when sleeping or leaving your room.
  • We shall not be liable for any stolen items. However, we are ready to assist the Police with any investigation. Please follow directives given to help secure your belongings.
  • You are not allowed to share your room keys with any other person.
  • If you misplace your room key, you would have to pay for a new key, or possibly pay for the entire lock depending on the circumstance.
  • Ironing on the bed or mattress is not permitted. Culprits will replace the mattress with a new one.
  • Hosting a visitor in your room is strictly prohibited. Doing so would lead to termination of contract without refund. Visitors must leave by 10pm.
  • Smoking in and around the hostel is strictly prohibited. Doing so would lead to ejection from the hostel without refund.
  • Any destruction or damage to hostel property, such as mattresses, curtains, walls, keys, and floors, shall be paid for by the student or students. It is prohibited to drive nails into or glue wallpaper to the walls and wardrobes.
  • Be vigilant when cooking. The gas cylinder must be turned off and the stove cleaned after use.
  • You are not permitted to use candles in the rooms. Rechargeable lamps are recommended if needed.
  • You must not leave unwashed utensils in the kitchen, corridors, or rooms. Rooms must be free from all kinds of garbage. All rubbish or garbage must be deposited into the bins provided.
  • You are expected to wash and hang clothes at the specified places allocated by management.
  • The hostel is strictly against assault of any kind on our staff and students. Doing so would lead to expulsion from the hostel without refund. Management will visit rooms at least once every month and will make every effort within 24 hours to get hostel problems fixed, including electricity, waste, water shortages, and gas.
  • You must report any suspicious behavior to management. It is not your responsibility to fight anyone suspected of being a criminal in the hostel. Report any suspected criminal caught to management or the Police.
  • It is an offence to misuse the washrooms. Ensure that you leave the washroom in a good state for your neighbour to use. Ensure your own safety and good health by adhering to all WHO COVID-19 protocols.

Property description
Jpounds Hostel is an ideal choice for comfortable and convenient student living. Located in the Alajo neighborhood of Accra. Located just a 10 minutes walk from Ghana Communication Technology University Main campus. The Hostel is clean and well-decorated, spacious rooms, laundry and cafeteria services. Every room is designed to give you a comfortable and serene environment to study, finish assignments, and unwind after a demanding day of lectures. The Hostel has the necessary facilities come equipped with a study area, a full kitchen, terraces, security lockers, and all the necessities for your fulfilment. The neighborhood shopping options includes Market, pharmacy, electronics, boutiques, hair salons, and more. You can relax, study, and socialise in our rooms with roommates who may end up being lifetime friends. Give it a shot.
